# Urgent rules (must go to a human, never counted as a normal theme)

Send to a human if the feedback involves ANY of the following:

1. **Data loss:** user says their decks, notes, cards, or progress disappeared or were deleted. (A reset streak is a bug, not data loss.)
2. **Privacy or security:** user can see someone else's data, someone else accessed their account, or credentials or personal information were exposed.
3. **Payments:** charged twice, charged after cancelling, or any billing error.
4. **Safety or harassment:** threats, harassment, or abuse from other users in study rooms or anywhere in the app. Figures of speech do not count.
5. **Legal or regulatory:** mentions a lawyer, lawsuit, regulator, or an accessibility complaint with legal action.

**Urgency**
- Data loss, privacy/security, safety/harassment, legal: urgent (same day).
- Payments: high (within 24 hours).

**Handoff note format:** Feedback ID, one-line summary, which rule matched, urgency, what the user asked for.
