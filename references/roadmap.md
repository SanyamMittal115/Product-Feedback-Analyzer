# StudySync roadmap (fictional), current version 4.1

## Shipped
- **Dark mode**: Settings > Appearance > Theme (Light / Dark / Auto). Shipped in 4.0.
- **Google Calendar sync**: Settings > Integrations.
- **Study timer (Pomodoro)**, **streaks**, **spaced repetition**.
- **Group study rooms** (text chat only) and shareable decks via link.
- **Android and iPhone apps.**
- **CSV import** for flashcard decks: Settings > Import. (Columns: front, back.)
- **Notification on/off toggles**, one per type (daily reminder, streak warning, room messages).

## Partial (exists, with known gaps)
- **Notifications:** only on/off per type. No choosing the time, no quiet hours.
- **Deck import:** CSV only. No direct Quizlet or Anki import.

## Planned
- **Offline mode for flashcards**: Q1 2027.
- **AI quiz and flashcard generation from notes or PDFs**: Q1 2027.
- **Apple Calendar and Outlook sync**: Q2 2027.

## Not planned (out of scope for now)
- **iPad-optimized app** (the phone app runs on iPad in a scaled layout).
- **Grade tracker / GPA calculator** (not a study-tool core feature).
- **Video or voice in study rooms** (users are pointed to Zoom or Discord).

## Known bugs
- **Android crashes when opening decks over about 500 cards.** Fix scheduled for 4.2.
